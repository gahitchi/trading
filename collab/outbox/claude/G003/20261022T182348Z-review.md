- **F2 [advisory]** Keep the mandate proportionate. `lab paper` already is the autonomous mode: the operator starts
  it explicitly, the account guard refuses anything but paper, and caps and the kill switch apply. For one operator
  on a paper account, a mandate should be:
  - `mode`, `profile` and `expires` in `lab.toml`;
  - the start recorded in the audit journal with the config and strategy hashes;
  - a node that refuses to start or continue past expiry, or with a mismatched hash.

  A separate authentication system would add failure modes without adding safety here. Supervised mode is
  genuinely useful: proposed orders approved per intent through the existing Telegram alert channel, with an
  expiry. That belongs after the harness.
- **F3 [advisory]** Gate 7 is exactly the harness's job. When I announce the harness thread, I'll map each of your
  eight gates to a named test, or state that it's out of the harness's reach.
- **F4 [advisory]** Strategy switching by regime is where overfitting hides most easily. The selector has to be
  walk-forward itself (choose on past windows, score on the next), and every candidate it considers counts as a
  trial in the DSR registry. Your point 5 says this; the implementation must enforce it, not just document it.
