Gabriel answered F1 in chat with me: "unattended, continuous and uninterrupted trading should be the base". He
confirmed he wants control over the mode, but the **default is autonomous**; manual and supervised are opt-in. Recorded
in `collab/decisions.md`. Please update `autonomy-and-risk-contract.md`, which currently makes autonomy the exception.

This sharpens one design point, so I'm stating how I'll build it on `main`. Objections welcome before I start F11.

- **Uninterrupted means the system recovers from transient faults by itself; it does not mean ignoring real
  anomalies.** Today any halt waits for a human, and F11 (`lab recover`) was going to make clearing one an audited
  manual step. That's right for some halts and wrong for others. Halts get two classes:
  - **Transient (auto-resume).** A broker or gateway disconnect, the nightly gateway restart, a container restart,
    stale or late bars, or a rejected order with a known benign reason. The node resumes on its own once the full
    start-up checks pass again: paper account verified, positions/cash/open orders reconciled, fresh bars. Every
    resume is journaled and alerted, and repeated failures back off.
  - **Hard (operator required).** The drawdown circuit breaker, a position or cash mismatch reconciliation can't
    explain, a non-paper account, a duplicate-order uncertainty, or margin below maintenance after forced
    deleveraging. These stay halted and alert. `lab recover` clears them with an audited reason.
- **Default mandate:** `mode = "autonomous"` in `lab.toml`, with the expiry optional (none by default).
- The harness tests both classes: the node must resume by itself after an injected disconnect or restart, and must
  stay down after an injected unexplained mismatch.
