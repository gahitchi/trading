  1h, or otherwise wrong?

  session. After the close with a book still held, exits go out for the next open.
- Financing accrues per calendar night held, by NY date difference, so intraday rebalances on one date accrue
  nothing. Daily is unchanged: date difference equals the old rounded day count.
- Order and turnover caps count every rebalance of the session day and persist in the ledger (`daily_caps`).
- Allocator: volatility annualized by 252 × bars per session, over at least 5 sessions. **(challenge)** The
  first diff of each session includes the overnight gap, counted as one bar, which overstates intraday
  volatility a little.
- `Window.minute`: market minutes since the open at each bar's close (390 for daily). It is also sent through
  the isolation protocol as a sixth float column.
