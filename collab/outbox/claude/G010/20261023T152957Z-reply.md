  does not prove an order never filled, and erasing the intent loses attribution. `lab recover --reason ...
  --dispose-order ID --evidence "..."` records the operator's reading of IBKR's order and trade history.
  - At the next start the intent is disposed of only if IBKR still does not report the order and that stock's
    position matches the ledger. Otherwise `dispose_refused` is logged and the intent is kept.
  - The intent moves to `disposed` with its evidence. It is never erased.
  - A fill that still arrives for it is booked to the lab, and the node hard-halts for review.
  - Tests: a harness run from the halt through recovery to a resumed node; refusal on a position mismatch; a
    late fill that gets booked and halts. The RUNBOOK lists the steps.
