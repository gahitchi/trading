How I propose we build it (objections welcome):

1. **Leverage is a sizing decision, not a source of edge.** Sharpe, DSR and PBO are leverage-invariant apart from
   financing and costs, so the statistical gates judge the strategy at 1x *net of borrow and financing*. The
   profile then picks gross and net exposure. That way no profile can push a strategy with no edge through the gates.
2. **`main` (me): long/short accounting in the core path**, tested through the F10 harness:
   - a margin account in the backtest instead of `AccountType.CASH`;
   - signed weights through Allocator, Risk and Execution (today `risk.py` clips to `max(0, w)`, and `config.py`
     rejects limits above 1);
   - gross as `Σ|q·p| / equity`, with net exposure reported separately;
   - daily accrual of borrow fees on shorts and interest on debit balances, and dividends charged on shorts;
   - a maintenance margin check that deleverages before the broker would;
   - reconciliation against the broker's margin figures;
   - the paper guard and kill switch unchanged.
3. **You: the profile table and the borrow/cost model, as a proposal for `main`.**
   - Profiles drive the real limits through `[risk] profile` in `lab.toml`: max gross, net range, max short gross,
     per-name caps and target vol. Aggressive must actually allow shorts and gross above 1x.
   - The ceiling is the broker's overnight requirement (Reg T for a standard margin account), not a constant in our
     code. A tighter operator setting still wins.
   - The borrow model needs a source for shortability and fee rates (please evaluate what IBKR publishes), a
     documented conservative default when data is missing (flagged in every result), and a rule that excludes
     hard-to-borrow names in backtests where we have no fee history.
   - Port the locate/fee/maintenance rules from `advanced_risk.py` into it.
4. **Delisting proceeds (C001-F1) become more important.** A short in a stock that delists to zero is a win, and a
   short in one acquired at a premium is a loss, so the data-driven proceeds I'm building cut both ways now.
