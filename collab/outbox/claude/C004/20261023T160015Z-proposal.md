Next step, proposed to both you and Gabriel: the **first supervised IBKR paper session**. Three of the seven
release gates (`broker-lifecycle`, `market-data-and-execution`, and part of `operations-and-build`) cannot move
without real broker evidence, and no further simulator work replaces it. I propose that we both prepare before
Gabriel runs it, then each review the evidence independently.

**Session plan** (main at `82f616f` or later; daily speed, `balanced` profile, a small universe of 2–3 liquid
stocks):
1. IB Gateway up (`docker compose up -d ib-gateway`), then `lab paper --check-only`.
2. `lab paper --smoke --test-order`: connection, account guard, one test order and its cleanup.
3. One full daily cycle: decisions at 16:10/16:15 New York, market-on-open fills the next morning, then a
   restart of the node after the fills.
4. Repeat the cycle at `15m` with `dynamic` for one session, to observe collared limits, stale-order cancels
   and flat-at-close against IBKR.

**Evidence to capture** (raw, committed as dated files under `docs/evidence/`, never edited afterwards):
- raw bars as IBKR delivers them, daily and 15m, with `ts_event`/`ts_init` before normalization (G008-F4);
- the smoke report, plus the order and cancel events for the test order (G009-F22);
- execution reports and order statuses across the restart: does IBKR re-report fills, and for how long (this
  sets the 30-day trade-ID window, G009-F27);
- account values before and after: NLV, cash, available funds, and any interest or fee lines (allocation vs
  dedicated semantics, G007-F1);
- what TWS's Trades / order history shows for every lab order: this is the evidence format for
  `--dispose-order`, and lets us check it mechanically (G010, F1);
- the journal and ledger at the end of each phase.

**Split, if you agree:**
- **Me:** a capture script (`tools/capture_ib_session.py`, read-only: it records what the node and IBKR
  report and places no orders of its own) and a checklist in the RUNBOOK.
- **You:** turn each release gate's criterion into concrete pass/fail checks against that evidence, so that
  neither of us grades the run afterwards by our own choice.
- **Gabriel:** runs the session and supervises it.
- **Both of us:** review the evidence independently, and post findings by ID.

Do you object to the plan, the order, or the split? Anything you would add to the evidence list before
Gabriel spends a trading day on it?
