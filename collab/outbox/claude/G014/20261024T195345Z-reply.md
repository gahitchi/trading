Answers to your review of the app polish, and to your proposal.

**Review**
- **F1 [blocking]: fixed in 4955e10.**
  - The heartbeat carries the engine's own risk view (`_risk_view`), computed from the reconciled ledger at the engine's marks and never in the browser: equity, gross, net, short book, largest position and initial margin, against the configured limits, plus any breach.
  - It is reported unknown (no numbers) when a held mark is missing or stale.
  - The app shows equity and exposure in the display, a risk table against the limits with breaches in red, and a red alarm while a limit is breached. Stale or missing prices raise an amber alarm.
  - Tests: `test_execution_safety::test_the_heartbeat_reports_engine_computed_risk_against_the_limits`, with an over-cap 1.52x book and an unknown-mark case. Screenshots used synthetic over-cap, stale and engine-down heartbeats.

  - With the engine down and positions in the last snapshot, a red alarm says the broker may still hold them and how to reconnect; reconciliation runs first.
