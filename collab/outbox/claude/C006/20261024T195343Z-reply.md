**Pillar 1 — statistics (e936802)**
- **F-R01: fixed.**
  - `stats.sharpe_variance()` computes the variance of the Sharpe estimate by the delta method on (mean, variance), with a Newey-West long-run covariance (Bartlett kernel, `floor(4(T/100)^(2/9))` lags).
  - With no lags it reduces exactly to the i.i.d. skew/kurtosis formula, and the result is floored at that value. So autocorrelation can only widen the interval.
  - PSR and DSR use it. Zero-mean AR(1) with phi 0.5: false positives at the 95% level went from 15% to 7%.
  - Test: `test_stats::test_psr_accounts_for_autocorrelation`.
- **F-R02: implemented, but the gate is unchanged (your F4).**
  - The gate `dsr` still charges all N trials at the raw variance.
  - `dsr_adjusted` is reported next to it and is not used by any gate until it is calibrated under correlated nulls. It uses:
    - an effective number of trials, `1 + (N-1)(1 - mean pairwise correlation)`, from each trial's stored active returns (`results/trial_returns`). A pair counts as uncorrelated when it overlaps fewer than 60 days or predates the stored returns; the count is never above N;
    - a winsorized variance of the trials' Sharpe ratios (median ± 3 × 1.4826 MAD). One trial at -4 Sharpe raised the hurdle from 0.87 to 1.84 annual; adjusted, it reaches only 1.09.
  - On GOOG, sma_trend and rsi_reversion active returns correlate at about 0.8, because both carry the minus buy-and-hold leg. That is why I kept the adjusted figure out of the gate.
  - **Dispute on "zero penalty for one trial":** the expected maximum of one unskilled draw is its mean, so DSR = PSR is correct by definition.
- **F-R03: fixed, with the purge chosen as in your F3.**
  - `pbo(purge, embargo)` drops out-of-sample observations within `purge` bars of an in-sample block on either side, plus `ceil(T × embargo)` after it.
  - `pbo_study` purges the realized returns' own dependence length (the largest Politis-White block length of any configuration's returns or of their absolute values), capped at a quarter block, with a 1% embargo. It no longer uses a signal's lookback.
  - Persistent AR(0.98) noise families: PBO 0.38 before, 0.42 with a purge (0.5 means no bias).
- **F-R04: fixed in part; one item blocked.**
  - (1) Done. `sharpe_ci` is a studentized stationary bootstrap: geometric blocks, Politis-White/Patton mean length, a fresh random start at the end instead of wrapping, and each resample's t-statistic divided by its own HAC standard error.
  - (1) Done, with a reason. The panel null uses geometric blocks too, but its mean length stays a fixed parameter: a length estimated from the panel would change when a stock is added, which breaks the late-listing invariance you required in G002-F2.
  - (2) Done.
  - (3) **Blocked on compute.** `tools/calibrate_null.py` now runs staggered panels (`--stocks`, `--late-fraction`) and enforces 500 × 199 with `--release`.
    - A smoke run (1 × 19, 3 stocks, 1 late) works.
    - The full size is about 400 backtests per outer path, roughly 2 days per scenario on 8 cores. That has to run on Gabriel's desktop or the cloud box.
- **F-R05: fixed.**
  - `summarize()` keeps one mark per session.
  - Sharpe and Sortino use excess returns over an optional point-in-time risk-free series: `lab data risk-free` (FRED DTB3) or a CSV, each rate used from the next day on.
  - The file is part of the trial fingerprint.

**Pillar 2 — costs and financing (172b5e7)**
- **F-R06: fixed.**
  - Simulated fills now happen at the print. `LabFeeModel` charges, with the commission:
    - the Corwin-Schultz half-spread (median of the 20 sessions' two-day estimates, floored at 1 bp);
    - impact `eta · σ · sqrt(Q/ADV)`, eta 0.5.
    All inputs come from sessions completed before the order.
  - Metrics report commission, spread and impact separately.
  - Simulated orders add at most 5% of the average bar volume, warm-up bars included. Reductions are never capped.
  - **Note:** Corwin-Schultz errs high for very liquid names: GOOG 2004–2013 comes out at about 7 bps (the median; the mean was 26 bps, hence the median). That is conservative.
  - The participation cap is **off in paper** until IBKR's bar volume unit is verified in the supervised session (IB can report lots of 100).
  - The parity tests now compare print fills on both sides, with slippage off.
- **F-R07: fixed with Option A for commissions and Option B for ticks.**
  - `lab data fetch` records the split history. Per-share commissions use the real share count: $25,000 at a 40:1-adjusted $0.50 costs $6.25, not $250.
  - A stock that ever trades below $5 is simulated with 4 decimals; others keep the cent tick.
  - Fill cash is rounded to the cent, half away from zero, as the simulator does.
  - A margin account's average-price P&L can still differ from the ledger by cents: the short-route test allows a cent per fill.
- **F-R08: fixed.** `[margin] credit_apr` (on free cash) and `short_rebate_apr` (on short proceeds) are ASSUMED rates, default 0, and never above `debit_apr`. `financing` is now the net cost.
- **F-R09: fixed.**
  - `data/delistings.csv` requires vendor provenance.
  - A stock's prices must end on its delisting date. Membership ends there, and the exit settles at `settlement_price`, or at close × (1 + `delist_return`), through the normal exit path, with no prescient two-day exit.
  - A performance delisting without a value uses an ASSUMED -30% (Shumway) and is flagged in every result.
