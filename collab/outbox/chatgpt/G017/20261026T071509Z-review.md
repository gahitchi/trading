This is a **logic-first source review**, not a claim of full CI or live market qualification. The owner requires economic and safety fundamentals to be correct before advanced models and the next Arch acceptance. Findings below apply to the main US equity lineage and the current unmerged development branch as specified.

- **F1 [blocking]** `src/lab/backtest.py:settle_delistings` fabricates two tradable post-delisting bars with `volume=float(df["volume"].max())` and floors zero settlement to `0.0001`. No actual post-delisting executable volume exists by construction. A corporate-action cash settlement must be a separate event with a true zero recovery allowed, not simulated broker liquidity. Prevent claims of realistic delisting execution until fixed.
- **F2 [blocking]** `src/lab/experiments.py:effective_risk` changes custom risk `max_weight=1.0, max_drawdown=0.9999` before research; the operator's real custom limits differ. `src/lab/research/worker.py` binds that overridden policy into the research deployment context while `paper.build_node` uses the configured risk. Tests explicitly assert the override. Research and deployment must evaluate the identical qualified risk policy or label the relaxed run non-deployable.
- **F3 [blocking]** `src/lab/agents/execution.py:_liquidity_cap` returns `None` (no cap) when configured participation lacks five observed volume bars. Missing evidence must deny added exposure, not silently disable a protective capacity constraint.
- **F4 [blocking]** `src/lab/backtest.py:run` passes `cfg.costs.max_participation` into ExecutionAgent, but `src/lab/paper.py:build_node` does not, defaulting paper capacity controls to `None`. Backtest and paper policy diverge, invalidating capacity parity.
- **F5 [blocking]** `src/lab/experiments.py:_compare` evaluates candidate backtests before `register_trial`. Although `research_state.record_research` records period exposure first, a crash after observing results but before candidate registration omits that attempted variant from DSR trial counts. Reserve per-candidate identities transactionally before evaluation.
- **F6 [blocking]** `src/lab/paper.py:build_node` performs approval checks only for `spec.path is not None`, leaving built-in paper strategies exempt. Distinguish explicitly labelled diagnostic paper trading from qualified autonomous demo policies; no strategy is automatically alpha-qualified by being built in.
- **F7 [advisory]** `src/lab/metrics.py:excess_returns` floors elapsed time with `astype("timedelta64[D]")`. Independent DST reproduction: US market closes Fri 2026-03-06 through Mon 2026-03-09 are 71 hours = 2.9583 days but are counted as 2 days. At 5% annual effective rate interest is 0.0003955236 instead of 0.0002673791 for the interval. Use nanosecond duration divided by 86400 seconds with DST tests.

Release qualification remains blocked. Preserve paper-only guards, unknown-state halts, D1/D2 partial, D3 blocked, D6 disabled. No autonomous new money should be admitted on the basis of synthetic order books, unverified funding or incomplete research calibration.
