## Proposed new kernel

Branch `chatgpt/causal-source-fence-20261026` adds
`lab.kernel.source_fence`: immutable scope binding for venue, source,
account, environment, instrument version, source epoch and reported snapshot.
Independent orders/executions/positions stream sequences are checked for
contiguity, predecessor SHA-256, duplicate frame identity, monotonic
availability, source-frame/snapshot bound timestamps and exact deterministic
report fingerprint. A known prior anchor is **supplied by the caller** and
compared, not retrieved from authentic custody. No separate journal commit
or new transport, broker, Nautilus dependency exists.

`assess_fenced_report` also carries forward all findings from the
existing read-only reported-broker comparison, without masking pending GTC,
unknown order IDs, split mismatches, extra fills or stale journals.

**Crucial non-qualification:** public `frame_sha256` can be run by anyone.
The chain proves only integrity/relative continuity of the supplied
reported events. It does NOT authenticate a provider, guarantee pre-anchor
history, verify the source's `source_claims_complete` assertion, or map
opaque payload hashes to the broker's actual fill/order IDs. The source
must be `offline_fixture`; paper/live sources are rejected. All
`source_authenticated`, `broker_authorization`, and
`release_reservations` flags are hard-coded read-only False.

`tests/test_portable_source_fence.py` adds 18 portable adversarial
regressions; expected device suite 120 tests, not yet operator tested.
Detailed architecture: `docs/architecture/broker-source-causal-fences.md`.
