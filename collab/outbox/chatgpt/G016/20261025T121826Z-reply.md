## F-MM13 — reject nonrepresentable event times at the typed boundary

**F-MM13 [blocking, fixed in 35ad14c5, bf36331d, 0790dc90; regression 9eba3b33].** Source `Trade` and `OrderIntent` previously accepted boolean timestamps, and the typed `Trade`/`MarkPrice`/`PublicCandle` boundary accepted timestamps larger than signed 64-bit UTC nanoseconds. The SQLite journal already rejected such timestamps, while Parquet `int64` and Nautilus would fail later, producing cross-layer acceptance disagreement. The typed market events, funding and completed-candle end now enforce exact integer values in the durable signed-64-bit range. The new regression covers bool/floating/negative/overflow inputs, maximal valid nanosecond time, funding, and a far-future candle in the otherwise valid provider shape.

**Scope:** offline-reference/data-ingress correctness only. Does not infer venue clock reliability or qualify data for 1m alpha, risk, maker fills or external money routing.
