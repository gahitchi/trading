## Findings (source evidence against G037 predecessor)

- **F1 [blocking]** A valid fill can move cumulative `filled` above the
  journaled order `qty` in `ExecutionAgent._apply_fill` without a
  `reconciliation_mismatch` halt or auditable anomaly latch.
  Repro: make an order of 10 shares and feed two distinct trade IDs
  for 8 + 3. G037 booked 11 with no overfill warning. G038 still books
  all 11 real reported shares, but persists `overfilled=true` and halts.
- **F2 [blocking]** `on_order_filled` could accept an invalid/mismatched
  report (no cash movement) and then call `_close_pending` when the
  broker cache said the order was closed. `_close_pending` could
  independently encounter an invalid fill but archive the journaled
  order anyway. Repro: mismatched SELL report for a journaled BUY,
  marked closed, followed by terminal cancel. G038 persists
  `invalid_fill=true`, stops terminal/disposal closure and retains
  write-ahead intent until audited reconciliation.
- **F3 [blocking]** The same `trade_id` reported against a different
  client order was silently accepted as a duplicate in both direct
  callbacks and historical cache replay. G038 hard-halts and refuses
  to use that ID as proof that a second order has settled.

## Accounting contract

- Overfills are **never clipped**, silently dropped or "repaired" by
  sending a fabricated offsetting order. The entire reported economic
  movement is captured once; any exposure breach must be investigated
  under the existing halt/reconciliation protections.
- Invalid or contradictory report -> no new monetary entry, no order
  intent erasure, persistent `invalid_fill` latch. The engine's
  paper guard and order/kill/risk authority are unchanged.
- Legacy applied IDs do not authenticate a source. A same-trade-ID/
  same-order collision that changes *price or quantity* remains an
  explicit unresolved gap because archived replay records currently
  lack immutable economic terms. This work is not broker-qualified.

## Requested independent review

1. Re-run split fills, terminal notifications before/after fills, a

2. Verify `_save()` precedes reporting any amount as booked, and a
   failed save latches `persistence_failed`; no local simulation
   state is confused for proven broker state.
3. Verify zero paper/live safety relaxations and no fictional
   balancing settlement.
