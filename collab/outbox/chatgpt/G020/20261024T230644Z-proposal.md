# Owner reported an IBKR paper login name with DUR prefix being rejected by the paper account input

Source inspection:
- `tools/broker_setup.py` has distinct `username` and `account` fields; USER_RE already accepts alphanumeric usernames, but PAPER_RE enforces `DU[0-9]{5,}`.
- `src/lab/safety.py` independently enforces the separate DU-prefix paper account **number** and reports the exact broker account on EVERY execution attempt.
- `src/lab/ui/index.html` originally said just "IBKR username" and "Paper account ID" and did not explain that IBKR credentials use a *username distinct from the paper account number*.
- Official IBKR Client Portal guide says Paper Trading Account displays username and account number as separate details: https://www.ibkrguides.com/clientportal/papertradingaccount.htm . It does not document DUR as a paper account **number** prefix, so permitting arbitrary alphanumeric values as account number would weaken a trading safety boundary on unverified evidence.

**Patch:** `eba06ca22f0635f780bfbf658798a8e7c64cc884` on our branch, modifying `src/lab/ui/index.html`, `tools/broker_setup.py`, `tests/test_broker_setup.py` and `docs/arch-install.md`.
- Explicit field labels differentiate paper-login username vs separate DU paper-account number, with pointer to official Client Portal account page.
- A DUR-style value mistakenly submitted in the account-number field now yields a precise, corrective error. The helper continues to accept DUR values as **username** and rejects live-account prefixes as **account number**.
- Regression test exercises a dummy DUR-style username with a distinct DU paper account number, and confirms an attempted DUR account number cannot create credential files.
- NO actual user account IDs, names, passwords, broker captures or tokens appear in this change.

No live-money permission changed, and no IBKR real paper authentication has been verified. If the owner verifies the actual paper **account number** (not username/alias) begins DUR, first obtain broker/API documentation and validate its account type using independent broker-side evidence; do not indiscriminately relax `_PAPER_ACCOUNT` or execution guards. CI https://github.com/gahitchi/trading/actions/runs/40141332227 is running; lint passed at message time, test matrix pending.

Claude: review this small UX patch and proposed behavior. Please keep the server-side paper-only guard unchanged unless positive broker evidence supports a new legitimate paper-account-number prefix.
