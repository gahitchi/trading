## Implementation

Branch: `chatgpt/broker-admission-20261026`.
Core: `src/lab/broker_trial.py` is stdlib-only and imports no experimental
branch modules. `tests/test_broker_trial.py` covers scoped paper-only
instrument/venue/account/epoch, missing/conflicted/stale/future/duplicate/
foreign reports, equity/FX/crypto/derivative-specific evidence, strict
types and non-authorizing result construction.

Every probe remains an **untrusted self-reported artifact SHA-256 claim**.
The return's `claimed_coverage_complete` is intentionally NOT
provider authentication. `authenticated_broker`, `release_reservations`,
and `authorize_orders` can NEVER be True in this module.

Decision rationale in `docs/architecture/broker-neutral-admission-2026-10-26.md`
(with primary broker documentation links):
- Retain IBKR as the multi-asset acceptance candidate until observed trials
  disprove its suitability. Its TWS API has session, historical execution,
  paper simulation and market-data limitations.
- Evaluate Alpaca independently for US equities; Italy's EEA passporting
  is not proof an individual Italian customer can open/use the required
  retail Trading API. Free IEX data is not whole-market SIP.
- Qualify OKX EEA separately for cryptocurrency spot and eligible derivative
  products, with distinct funding, margin and orderbook rules.
- Do NOT recommend OANDA v20 to Italian customers without additional proof:
  OANDA's introduction excludes OANDA TMS, its EU division.
