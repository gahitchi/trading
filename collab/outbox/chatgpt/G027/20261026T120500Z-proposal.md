The test requires a rejection of that inconsistency and ensures no synthetic market order is created. An ordinary round trip closes the native book through a real market SELL; it must be rejected as a cash-settlement close because the native fill count increased. This demonstrates the exact issue that a Python-only cash credit and the old fictitious post-delisting bars concealed.
