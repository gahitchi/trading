# Owner's two product requirements and execution plan

Gabriel has clarified that the product's **primary** goals are (1) advanced trading strategies whose permitted techniques, long/short use, leverage and exposure adapt to selectable risk profiles and (2) **fully autonomous trading only when the user explicitly requests it**. We must build these, not spend the next iteration on disconnected research panels. Paper accounts remain the absolute protocol constraint.

This branch implements research-profile tables with signed net/gross/short bounds and optional per-profile volatility targeting, fixes the distorted cross-asset null calibration caused by late listings, and adds `docs/architecture/autonomy-and-risk-contract.md` spelling out manual/supervised/autonomous mandates and acceptance tests. **No autonomous broker order capability is claimed.**

Proposed responsibility split and dependency graph:
1. **Claude on main:** complete F10 real sandbox-node event/fill harness, F11 audited `lab recover`, and signed long/short/margin core with full borrow, fees, dividends, margin and safety reconciliation. Preserve paper-only guards.
2. **ChatGPT on owned branches:** supply/test profile-based exposure and permissions contract, conditional activation bound to account/strategy/profile hashes, and data/borrow evidence rules. No permissions bypass. Use your harness once it is available.
3. **Joint:** adaptively choose *qualified* trading families (trend, mean reversion, market-neutral pair/stat-arb) with walk-forward/holdout/noise gates and as-of regime rules. Leverage is a risk sizing choice, not proof of statistical edge.
4. **Joint release gate:** automated/manual/supervised mode isolation; authenticated opt-in/expiry; idempotent pending orders across restarts; reconciled positions/cash; kill switch and independent flatten; chaos tests for rejected locates, recalls, margin calls, gaps, fill races and stale bars.
