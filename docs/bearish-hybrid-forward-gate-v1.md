# Bearish hybrid: measured-time OI controls and read-only forward audit

**Research-only, zero executable notional.** This change corrects a sample-identity weakness in the public forward OI collector. Capturing a response at a new time does not mean that the exchange measured OI again. Two receipts that refer to the same measurement timestamp must not be treated as two independent observations. Reusing the same measurement with a different value indicates a conflict and blocks evaluation. Duplicates with an unchanged measurement/value do not refresh data age. Out-of-order measurements fail closed, as do mixed instrument receipts.

The `first_seen_oi helper checks exchange-reported **measurement identity and timing** separately from locally received availability. Measurement times must advance at least five minutes between the two samples whose OI change is compared, in addition to the existing five-to-sixty minute separation in receipt times. A measurement older than two minutes **when received** is conservatively excluded as stale (this is a research policy, not a claim about Binance endpoint SLAs). The original HTTP response-received time remains the only permissible `previous_ns` or `observed_ns` value used by downstream signal research; nominal measurement times never backdate publication.

The new `inspect_public_features function offers a read-only, single-minute diagnostic that can combine a caller-provided closed `Bar` with independently stored OI/funding receipts. It never makes a network request. It returns a `ForwardDiagnostic` with an explicit `HybridObservation`, receipt counts and the following fail-closed invariant: **`market=None`, `funding_verified=False`, `source_complete=False`, `qualified=False`, `actual_notional=0.0`**. In particular, complete historical price ZIP files and funding history responses do not prove that a candle or settlement was observable at that earlier minute. The result is for data-quality inspection, *not* funded replay or a paper/live signal. A dynamic equity replay refuses its incomplete source flag.

The gateway does not repair the unresolved acquisition limitations: local clocks and HTTPS responses are not independent source signatures, historical listing and 30-day liquidity are unknown, funding completeness is unverified, short execution is still only OHLC research and the previously protected BTC holdout cannot be used. All research families and parameters must still be preregistered before an economic study. No actual data was downloaded or backtested in this change.

Tests are synthetic and cover same-measurement repeats and conflicts, stale/out-of-order samples, cross-instrument receipts, as-of nonleakage, and the always-unqualified diagnostic flags. Run `PYTHONPATH=src:. python -m pytest tests/test_bearish_hybrid_forward_gate.py tests/test_bearish_hybrid_public_capture.py -q`, Ruff checks, then the complete suite.
