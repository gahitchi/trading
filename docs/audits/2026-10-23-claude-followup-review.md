## Assessment

Claude made substantial improvements: the shared live/backtest architecture remains intact; the original negative-equity exit, future-bar intake, null donor leakage, date-based holdout guard, malformed controls, health reporting and Agent-trailer defects now have meaningful regressions. Profile behavior follows Gabriel's C003 decision. The intraday harness exercises the real paper node rather than an imitation of its strategy code.

The implementation remains a research and supervised paper-testing prototype. It is **not qualified for unattended trading or adaptive strategy deployment**. This turn found defects in the recovery, isolation, approval and intraday additions. The proposed fixes materially narrow those defects, but broker economics, execution realism, pending aggregate risk and statistical qualification remain incomplete. No profitability or real-money readiness is established.

This is a source review and fault-oriented regression audit, not formal verification or a security certification. I reviewed Claude's changes and their interactions with execution, research, configuration, persistence, simulation and deployment; I did not exercise every possible state. Findings include regressions against my own earlier work. The earlier G007 report is preserved unchanged as historical evidence.

The Linux filter is a shared-kernel boundary, not a VM. It deliberately has no insecure fallback on unsupported platforms or installation failure. This environment exposes UID 0 without permission to change groups; the baseline worker failed legitimate signals there. The new worker still attempts credential dropping, tolerates that specific host restriction, and requires the syscall filter before generated source executes. Docker continues running the lab unprivileged. Only Linux x86-64 was exercised locally; architecture portability remains a CI/deployment obligation.

Additional small changes: expose `BorrowBook.rate_held()` as requested in G006-F4; correct stale profile-limit documentation, describe the approval migration and fixed holdout, and remove the promise that flat-at-close guarantees no overnight position.

## Remaining priorities for Claude

1. **Broker and outstanding-order state:** qualify actual IB reported cash, net liquidation, available funds and account ownership; implement durable explicit disposition of unknown orders. Reserve worst-case pending aggregate exposure and margin across partial fills. The local simulator does not enforce broker margin or independently settle financing.
2. **Smoke and exit reliability:** use a durable order/recovery protocol for test orders and cleanup; test partial fill plus cancellation, late fills, process death, repeated rejected exits and reconnects. Existing immediate cleanup cannot justify an unattended guarantee.
3. **Market/execution realism:** add an exchange calendar with holidays, early closes and halts; capture real adapter timestamps. Intraday backtest uses next opening prints while live entries use collared limits. The harness has a known extra-bar difference and no intraday fill-for-fill parity test. Market orders have no volume constraint. Until reconciled, intraday returns are exploratory.
4. **Research validity:** delisting/terminal proceeds and point-in-time membership (G007-F17), walk-forward identity/carry/switching costs (F19), trial families and complete artifact/data/protocol/environment manifests (F8/F25). Borrow history still does not establish live locates, inventory size, recalls or intraday publication times (F21). ChatGPT still owns null-of-null calibration (F18/G002); no calibration is claimed here.
5. **Operational durability and product scope:** durable alerts and disk-failure handling (F23), reproducible locked builds (F24), account-wide single-writer ownership rather than only a runtime-directory lock (F15). A qualified adaptive strategy bank and manual/supervised authority modes are still future work (F20).

On C003's challenges: rounding a 15-minute delay up to an hourly bar is extra latency, but **not a mathematical lower bound on profitability**; later entries can help or hurt. Including the overnight return in one intraday observation does not by itself prove annualized variance is overstated: define whether the risk target includes overnight exposure, then compare estimators on a known process. Neither uncertainty was “fixed” by guessing a favorable formula.
