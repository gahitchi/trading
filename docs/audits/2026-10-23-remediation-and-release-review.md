## Additional defect found and corrected

**G010-F1:** the backtest engine stops at its last market event. Its valuation callback one second later may never run, leaving the reported curve without the last day's mark and fills. `backtest.run` now explicitly values the final book, refuses unavailable held marks, and includes final financing. `test_final_backtest_equity_contains_last_mark_and_last_fills` independently reconstructs cash plus marked positions from executions. This changes historical metrics; old results must not be represented as results of the new engine.

The new reservation path also exposed a simulator representation difference: Nautilus rounds realized partial-close P&L to USD cents, whereas the ledger sums execution cash. The long/short fixture accumulates $0.01 of rounding across weighted-average close-outs. The test now allows that measured cent **and** independently checks exact execution-cash equity to 1e-6. No production reconciliation tolerance was loosened.

## Release standard and handoff

`python3 tools/release_check.py` currently returns exit 1 and `ready: false`, with seven explicit blocked gates in `docs/release-criteria.json`. This is intentional. The checklist is a review aid, not automatic certification. It does not disable development or claim that changing a JSON status makes the software safe.

Claude should review this exact SHA before integrating, reproduce the regressions, answer every remaining finding by ID, and prioritize:

1. Broker lifecycle, execution-cost/gap reservations, cash/fee semantics, bounded durable trade-ID history and evidence-based recovery/migration.
2. Data/adapter/session parity and the statistical calibration protocol, including failed and adaptive selections.
3. Explicit adaptive bank and operating-mode authority, classified-flow reporting, pinned image/OS build provenance, supervision/soak and independent isolation review.

Do not mark development finished while any blocking gate remains. If a requested feature is deferred, record an explicit owner-approved scope decision; do not close its finding by relabelling it as a test limitation.
